Privacy Policy

Settle (www.settle0.in.net) · Last updated 8 November 2026

Settle is a personal app for splitting expenses with friends and keeping track of loans, bills, credit cards, meals and bets. This page explains what data Settle keeps, why, and what it never does with it.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your profile picture if you sign in with Google.
  • What you enter: groups, expenses, loans, bills, payments, UPI IDs, meals, trips, discussions and other things you add to the app.
  • Gmail data (only if you connect Gmail): see the next section.

Gmail access

Connecting Gmail is optional. If you do, Settle asks for read-only access (gmail.readonly) and uses it only for features you turn on:

  • Credit Cards: finds your credit card statement emails and reads the amount due, due date and transactions from them.
  • Expenses: finds payment and transaction emails so you can add them as expenses.
  • My bets: finds your bookmaker's bet-history export emails and imports the bets in them.

Settle searches only for those kinds of emails. It never sends, deletes, changes or labels any email, and it doesn't read your other mail. Your Gmail access token is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect Gmail at any time in the app. You can also remove Settle's access in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.

Settle's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is used only to provide the features above to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person reads it except where you ask for help or the law requires it.

AI processing

Some features use AI services (Groq and OpenRouter) to read text you give them: a statement's text to pull out the figures, a meal or expense you type or say, or a photo of a meal you upload. Only the text or image needed for that one request is sent, and the answer comes back to Settle. Settle doesn't use your data to train any AI model.

Who can see your data

  • People you share a group, loan, bill or discussion with see what's in it, as in any expense-splitting app.
  • The app's administrator, who runs the server.
  • Nobody else. Settle doesn't sell or rent your data and has no advertising.

Storage and security

Data is kept on Settle's own server and sent over HTTPS. Passwords, Gmail tokens and bank statement passwords are stored hashed or encrypted.

Deleting your data

You can delete what you've added from inside the app, and disconnect Gmail at any time. When you disconnect Gmail, its stored token is deleted. To have your whole account deleted, ask the app's administrator.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
